## How much is Serious Needs Assistance?
It is a one-time payment of $770 per household. The amount is adjusted every year.

## What is it for?
Emergency supplies like water, food, first aid, breastfeeding supplies, infant formula, diapers, personal hygiene items, or fuel for transportation.

## Who may qualify?
You must meet all of these:
- You are a U.S. citizen, non-citizen national, or qualified non-citizen.
- FEMA can confirm your identity.
- Your main home is in the declared disaster area, and you live there most of the year.
- Disaster damage is confirmed by an inspection or by documents you send.
- You apply during the availability window.
- You report displacement, a need for shelter, or emergency costs on your application.
FEMA decides.

## How long is it available?
For 30 days after the disaster declaration. FEMA may extend it to 60 days if a state, territory, or Tribal Nation asks in writing. In the hardest-hit areas, FEMA may pay before inspections are done.

## What if I am denied?
You get a letter that explains why. You can appeal with more documents that show your identity, residency, or repair and cleanup needs. Receipts, contractor estimates, or landlord papers can help.

## How do I send documents?
Apply at DisasterAssistance.gov. Send documents by uploading online, mail, fax to 1-800-827-8112, or visit a Disaster Recovery Center.
