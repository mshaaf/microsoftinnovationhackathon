## What is the Individuals and Households Program?
The Individuals and Households Program (IHP) gives money and direct services to eligible people affected by a disaster. It is for people who have necessary expenses and serious needs that insurance or other help does not cover.

## What kinds of help does IHP offer?
- Money for temporary housing (rent or hotel costs) when you cannot live in your main home.
- Money to repair or replace an owner-occupied main home, including a privately owned road or path to it.
- Temporary housing units, if rental housing is not available.
- Money for hazard mitigation, to help homeowners rebuild stronger.
- Money for other disaster expenses that insurance does not cover.

## Does IHP replace insurance?
No. The page says IHP help "is not a substitute for insurance and cannot compensate for all losses caused by a disaster."

## Who decides, and what does FEMA check?
FEMA decides who may qualify and how much they get. FEMA requires a home inspection and proof of ownership and occupancy. You can appeal a FEMA decision. To let FEMA share your records with another person or group, you need a Privacy Act consent form (FF-104-FY-21-118).

## What other help is there?
Disaster Unemployment Assistance, Crisis Counseling, and Disaster Legal Services may also be available.
