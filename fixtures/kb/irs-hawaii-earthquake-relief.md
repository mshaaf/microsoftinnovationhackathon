## Who may get IRS tax relief for the Hawaii County earthquake?
The IRS says affected taxpayers include people who live in Hawaii County, businesses whose main place of business is in Hawaii County, and tax-exempt organizations in the area. Relief workers and people visiting the area who were killed or injured are also listed. People outside the area may qualify if their records needed to meet a deadline are in the disaster area. The IRS decides.

## What is the new tax deadline?
The IRS moved deadlines that fell between May 22, 2026, and February 1, 2027, to February 1, 2027. You can file returns and pay taxes that were due in that time by then. One exception: payments for 2025 individual income tax returns that were due April 15, 2026, do not get this relief.

## What is postponed?
Postponed items include individual, corporate, and estate or trust income tax returns. Partnership, S corporation, and trust returns are also postponed. So are estate and gift tax returns, employment and excise tax returns, returns of tax-exempt organizations, and estimated income tax payments.

## What is not postponed?
W-2 and 1094-1099 series forms, and Forms 1042-S, 3921, 3922, and 8027, are not postponed. Employment and excise tax deposits are not postponed either. Deposit penalties due May 22 to June 8, 2026 will be abated if the deposits were made by June 8, 2026.

## How do I ask the IRS for relief if I am outside the disaster area?
Call the IRS special services line at 866-562-5227. Tax practitioners with ten or more clients should use the IRS bulk disaster relief procedures.

## What other IRS help is listed?
The IRS waives the fee for copies of previously filed returns; use FEMA declaration number 4936-DR. Qualified disaster relief payments are excluded from gross income. Special retirement distribution options use Form 8915-F. Casualty loss deductions are available for the current or prior year. Payment agreements stay in effect even if you miss payments during the postponement.

## Where can I get free tax help?
VITA and TCE free tax help: 800-906-9887. AARP Tax-Aide: 888-227-7669. Other disaster resources are at disasterassistance.gov.
