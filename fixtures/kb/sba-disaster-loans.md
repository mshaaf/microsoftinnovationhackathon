## Who may apply for an SBA disaster loan?
SBA lists businesses of all sizes, homeowners, renters, and private nonprofit organizations. SBA decides who is approved.

## What kinds of SBA disaster loans are there?
Physical damage loans for homes, personal property, and businesses. Economic Injury Disaster Loans (EIDL) give working capital for ordinary business expenses. Military Reservist EIDL is for businesses that lose essential employees to active duty. Mitigation help can raise a physical damage loan by up to 20% for protection against future risk.

## How much can a home or personal property loan be?
Homeowners: up to $500,000 to repair or replace a primary home. Renters and homeowners: up to $100,000 for personal property such as clothing, furniture, cars, and appliances. Insurance money is subtracted from the eligible amount.

## What are the terms?
For people who cannot get credit elsewhere, the interest rate will not exceed 4%. The first payment is deferred 12 months with no interest during that time. Terms can be up to 30 years. There is no prepayment penalty.

## What about business loans?
Business physical disaster loans go up to $2 million. They cover real property, machinery, equipment, fixtures, inventory, and leasehold improvements. For businesses that can get credit elsewhere, the interest rate will not exceed 8%. Physical and EIDL loans combined are limited to $2 million.

## How do I apply for an SBA disaster loan?
Online: lending.sba.gov/search-disaster/. Phone: 800-659-2955. In-person appointments: appointment.sba.gov/. Other questions: DCS@sba.gov.
