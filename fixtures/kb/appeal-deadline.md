## Can I appeal FEMA's decision anytime?
No. There is a deadline. You must appeal within 60 days of the date on your decision letter.

## Where do I find the 60-day date?
The 60 days count from the date shown on your FEMA decision letter. Check the date printed on the letter.

## What happens if I miss the deadline?
The page says the deadline is firm: you must appeal within 60 days. FEMA decides on any appeal. If you are close to the deadline, contact FEMA at 1-800-621-3362.
