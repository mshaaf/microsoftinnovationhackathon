## Does the IRS give tax relief after disasters?
Yes. The IRS page lists tax relief for people and businesses affected by disasters, organized by state and date.

## Is there relief for the Hawaii earthquake?
The page lists Hawaii earthquake relief with tax deadlines postponed to Feb. 1, 2027. Several other 2026 disasters also list a Feb. 1, 2027 date.

## Where do I find out if my area is covered?
Visit the IRS "Around the nation" page for eligible localities and details for each disaster. The IRS also has FAQs for disaster victims that explain who counts as an affected taxpayer.
