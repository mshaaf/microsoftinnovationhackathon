## What can FEMA help with after the Kona earthquake (DR-4936-HI)?
FEMA Individual Assistance may cover:
- Temporary housing for homeowners and renters who cannot live in their homes because of disaster damage.
- Home repairs to restore basic safety and function.
- Repair or replacement of water catchment systems.
- Other needs such as personal property, transportation, and medical costs.

## How do I apply?
There are three free ways:
- Online at DisasterAssistance.gov
- With the FEMA mobile app
- By phone at 1-800-621-3362 (7 a.m. to 10 p.m. HST, 7 days a week)

## What is the deadline?
Applications must be submitted by November 1, 2026 to qualify for FEMA help for this earthquake.

## Does the SBA offer loans too?
Yes. The SBA offers disaster loans with fixed interest rates and an automatic 12-month payment deferment with 0% interest for the first 12 months.

## Is there language help or in-person help?
FEMA offers free translation through its helpline. Local Recovery Centers will open in Hawaii County. Check ready.hawaii.gov for locations and hours.
