## Does FEMA only give one payment?
No. Help depends on your situation and may include more than one check.

## Can I get a hotel paid by calling 1-800-621-3362?
No. The page says calling that number cannot secure hotel payment directly. You apply through DisasterAssistance.gov.

## Does FEMA charge fees?
No. FEMA never charges fees for applications or inspections.

## Can FEMA take my property or land?
No. FEMA cannot seize your property or land. Applying for help does not give the government any ownership rights.

## Does FEMA hand out vouchers?
No. You can apply through DisasterAssistance.gov, by phone, or at Disaster Recovery Centers.

## Can I still apply if I have insurance?
Yes. You can apply for help with expenses your insurance does not cover. Homeowners insurance does not normally cover flood damage. Flood coverage needs a separate policy.

## Does FEMA pay weekly rates for storm cleanup?
No.

## How do I report a suspected scam?
Call 866-720-5721 or email StopFEMAfraud@fema.dhs.gov.
