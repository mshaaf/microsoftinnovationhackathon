## What does FEMA help with?
FEMA may help with temporary housing costs, basic home repairs, or other essential disaster-related needs that insurance does not cover.

## What is an SBA disaster loan?
The U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA) works with FEMA. It is the largest source of federal disaster recovery money for rebuilding, replacing, or repairing disaster-damaged homes.

## Do I need a business to apply to the SBA?
No. You do not need to own a business. Low-interest disaster loans are available to homeowners and renters in declared major disaster areas.

## What can an SBA loan pay for?
Home repair or replacement, personal property, vehicles, and mitigation. It can also cover business losses and working capital for small businesses and nonprofits.

## Do I have to take a loan if approved?
No. If you are approved for a loan, you do not have to accept it. Money you borrow must be repaid over time.
