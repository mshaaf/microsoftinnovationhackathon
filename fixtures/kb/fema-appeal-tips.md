## What are the deadlines for an appeal?
You have 60 days from the date on your FEMA decision letter to submit an appeal. After you appeal, expect a decision in about 90 days at most.

## What should be on every page?
Your full name, current phone number and address, the disaster number, your FEMA application number, and the address of the damaged home. The FEMA application number should be on every page.

## What papers help my appeal?
Receipts, bills, repair estimates, property titles or deeds, and anything else that supports your reasons. Receipts and bills should show the business name and contact information so FEMA can verify them.

## What form do I need?
The letter includes an Appeal Request Form. Sign and complete it. If someone else writes your appeal letter, they must sign it, and you must also sign an Authorization for Release of Information form.

## How do I send it?
1. Upload to your DisasterAssistance.gov account.
2. Mail to FEMA, P.O. Box 10055, Hyattsville, MD 20782-8055.
3. Fax to 800-827-8112.
4. Visit a Disaster Recovery Center (search by ZIP at FEMA.gov/DRC).

## How do I reach FEMA?
The FEMA Helpline is 800-621-3362, 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. CT daily. Press 2 for Spanish. Relay and captioned phone services are accepted.
