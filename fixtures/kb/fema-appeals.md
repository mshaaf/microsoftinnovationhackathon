## How long do I have to appeal?
You must appeal within 60 days of the date on your FEMA decision letter.

## What should I include?
- Your FEMA application number and disaster number on every page.
- Documents for the decision you are appealing, such as repair receipts, bills, or estimates.
- Optional: the appeal form from your letter, or a letter that explains your situation.
- If someone submits for you, include their signed authorization.

## How do I send my appeal?
- Online: log in to DisasterAssistance.gov and use the Correspondence "Upload Center."
- In person: visit a Disaster Recovery Center (egateway.fema.gov/ESF6/DRCLocator).
- Mail: FEMA - Individuals & Households Program National Processing Service Center, P.O. Box 10055, Hyattsville, MD 20782-8055.
- Fax: (800) 827-8112, Attention: FEMA - Individuals & Households Program.

## How long until I hear back?
Decisions usually are made within 30 days, but can take up to 90 days. You get a written notice by mail or in your DisasterAssistance.gov account. FEMA may ask for more information.

## Who can I call with questions?
The FEMA Helpline: 1-800-621-3362. Video relay and captioned phone services are accepted.
