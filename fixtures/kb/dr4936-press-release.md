## Who can ask for help for the Kona earthquake?
Federal disaster help is available for Hawaii County residents affected by the May 22, 2026 Kona Earthquake (DR-4936-HI). The declaration date is September 1, 2026. FEMA decides who qualifies.

## What is the registration deadline?
November 1, 2026.

## How do I apply?
- Phone: 1-800-621-3362 (1-800-621-FEMA), 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. HST, 7 days a week. Relay services are accepted.
- Online: DisasterAssistance.gov
- Mobile: the FEMA app on a smartphone or tablet

## What kinds of help may FEMA give?
- Temporary housing for homeowners and renters
- Basic repairs to primary homes
- Other Needs Assistance: personal property, transportation, medical and dental costs, childcare

## What should I do about insurance?
If you have homeowners or renters insurance, file a claim as soon as possible. By law, FEMA cannot duplicate benefits for losses covered by insurance.

## Where can I learn about SBA loans?
Go to sba.gov/disaster, call 800-659-2955, or email disastercustomerservice@sba.gov.
