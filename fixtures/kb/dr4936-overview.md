## What is DR-4936-HI?
It is the federal disaster declaration for the Hawaii earthquake (Kona Earthquake). The incident period is May 22, 2026. The declaration date is September 1, 2026. Federal help is available for people who may qualify.

## How do I apply?
The fastest way is DisasterAssistance.gov. You can also use the FEMA mobile app or call the FEMA Helpline at 800-621-3362.

## What if I have insurance?
File a claim with your insurance company right away. FEMA assistance cannot help with losses already covered by insurance.

## Where else can I get support?
The page lists:
- Hawaii Emergency Management Agency
- City and County of Honolulu Department of Emergency Management
- American Red Cross of Hawaii
- The 24/7 Disaster Distress Helpline (SAMHSA)
- HUD Housing Counseling Services
- Help in Spanish, Chinese, Vietnamese, and American Sign Language
