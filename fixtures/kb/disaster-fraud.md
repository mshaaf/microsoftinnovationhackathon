## How do I know a FEMA inspector is real?
Real inspectors always wear an official government badge. They never ask for your nine-digit registration number or your bank details. FEMA inspections are free.

## Will anyone charge me for FEMA help?
No. Federal agencies will never charge you for disaster assistance or for help filling out an application.

## How do I avoid contractor fraud?
Use licensed contractors with references you have checked. Do not pay more than half of the repair cost up front.

## What if I get FEMA mail I did not ask for?
Criminals may apply for FEMA help using stolen personal information. If you get FEMA letters you did not request, contact the FEMA helpline at 800-621-3362 (7 a.m. to 11 p.m. ET, daily).

## How do I report fraud?
Contact FEMA's Investigations and Inspections Division:
- Email: StopFEMAFraud@fema.dhs.gov
- Fax: 202-212-4926
- Helpline: 800-621-3362

The page also lists the National Center for Disaster Fraud and the DHS Office of the Inspector General.
