## What housing help may FEMA offer?
- Rent: money for housing if the disaster forced you out of your home.
- Lodging expense reimbursement: emergency hotel or motel costs.
- Home repair or replacement.
- Accessibility needs: home changes for a disability.
- Privately owned roads, bridges, and docks, when damage blocks access to your main home.
- Hazard mitigation: money for measures to help eligible homeowners repair or rebuild stronger, more durable homes.

## What is Serious Needs help?
It is an upfront, flexible payment per household for essentials like food, water, baby formula, breastfeeding supplies, medication, and other serious disaster-related needs.

## What other needs may FEMA help with?
- Temporary housing options if you are displaced
- Child care costs caused by the disaster
- Home cleaning and sanitizing
- Repair or replacement of appliances, furniture, and computers
- Medical and dental costs from disaster injuries
- Funeral or reburial costs
- Group flood insurance (3 years, for Special Flood Hazard Areas)
- Vehicle repair or replacement
- Moving and storage costs

## What rules apply?
You must be a U.S. citizen, non-citizen national, or qualified alien. FEMA must be able to verify your identity using public records. Insurance or other help must not cover your need. The expense must be directly caused by the declared disaster. FEMA decides.
