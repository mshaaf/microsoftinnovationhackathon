## Who may apply for an SBA home and personal property loan?
Homeowners and renters in declared disaster areas may apply for help to recover from damaged property. SBA decides who is approved.

## How much can I borrow?
Homeowners: up to $500,000 to replace or repair a primary residence. Renters and homeowners: up to $100,000 to replace or repair personal property such as clothing, furniture, cars, and appliances.

## What can the loan pay for?
Disaster losses that insurance does not fully cover. You may not use the funds to upgrade a home or add to it, unless the local building code requires it. Funds cannot replace lost profits. You may also qualify for extra mitigation funding equal to 20% of verified damages.

## What are the interest rate and payment terms?
For people unable to get credit elsewhere, the fixed interest rate will not exceed 4%. The first payment is deferred for 12 months, with no interest for those 12 months. The loan can last up to 30 years. There is no prepayment penalty or fee.

## Do I need collateral?
Collateral is required for loans over $50,000 in Presidential declarations. SBA will ask what collateral you have, but will not decline a loan for lack of collateral.

## How do I apply?
Call SBA at 800-659-2955 or apply through the SBA online lending portal.
