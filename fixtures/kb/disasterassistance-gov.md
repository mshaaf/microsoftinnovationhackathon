## How do I apply for disaster help?
You can apply online at DisasterAssistance.gov. You can also log in to the site to check the status of your application. Deadlines are different for each disaster, so check the page for your state's declared disaster.

## What is the FEMA Helpline number and when is it open?
The FEMA Helpline is 1-800-621-3362. It is open 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. in your time zone, 7 days a week. Hours may be longer when things are very busy. The line accepts video relay service (VRS), captioned telephone (CTS), and other services.

## What other help is listed on the site?
The site links to the Red Cross shelter locator and emergency shelter lists, FEMA Disaster Recovery Centers, state emergency management offices, and SBA low-interest disaster loans for businesses and homeowners. It also has information about disaster rumors and scams.

## What languages does the site support?
English, Spanish, Haitian Creole, Chinese, Vietnamese, Russian, Arabic, Korean, Portuguese, German, and Filipino. There is also an American Sign Language video called "What to Expect Before Applying for FEMA Assistance."

## What if I missed the deadline?
Deadlines vary by disaster. The site says late applications may be accepted for some past disasters. FEMA decides.
