## How will FEMA contact me after I apply?
FEMA may email you from noreply-ecorr@dhs.gov, call you from 1-800-621-3362, or text you from 43362. Check your DisasterAssistance.gov account often for status changes.

## Will my application status change?
Yes. FEMA looks at your application for several types of help, and your status may change a few times. You can see progress by logging in to your online account.

## What might FEMA ask me for?
FEMA may ask you to:
- Prove who you are, with Social Security documents or valid identification.
- Show you owned or lived in the home at the time of the disaster.
- Have a home inspection, in person or remote, to document damage.

## How do I send FEMA documents?
You can upload them online, mail them to FEMA's Hyattsville address, or fax them to 1-800-827-8112.

## What if my application says it has not been approved?
Read your FEMA letter for the specific reasons. You have the right to appeal a denial through FEMA's appeals process. FEMA makes the decision.
