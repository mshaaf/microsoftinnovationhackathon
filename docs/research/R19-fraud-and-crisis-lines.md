# R19: Fraud guidance and crisis lines

Checked 2026-09-23.

## Fraud copy (from FEMA fraud pages)
- FEMA, SBA, and other federal disaster workers **never ask for money** and never charge for help applying or for an inspection.
- FEMA inspectors won't ask for your Social Security number or bank information.
- FEMA staff won't ask for your 9-digit registration number. They already have it.
- To check whether a FEMA call or email is real: FEMA Helpline 1-800-621-3362.
- Report fraud: FEMA Disaster Fraud Hotline **1-866-720-5721**, or StopFEMAfraud@fema.dhs.gov.

Source: https://www.fema.gov/assistance/individual/disaster-fraud and FEMA press release 2026-07-17 "Beware of Disaster Fraud".

## Handoff card numbers
| Card | Numbers |
|---|---|
| Emergency | **911** first |
| Shelter / unsafe housing | **211**, FEMA Helpline 1-800-621-3362 |
| Sensitive: distress after disaster | Disaster Distress Helpline **1-800-985-5990** (call or text, 24/7, Spanish available) |
| Sensitive: self-harm | **988** Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text) |
| Sensitive: domestic violence | National Domestic Violence Hotline **1-800-799-7233** (text START to 88788) |

Sources: samhsa.gov/find-help/helplines/disaster-distress-helpline, 988lifeline.org, thehotline.org.
